## Publication gate

**split-public-private**

理由不是要你现在立刻清数据，而是长期结构上更合理：

```text
public code + validated Public Archive
                │
                └── public

raw exports + full personal evidence
                │
                └── private/local when owner later authorizes
```

现有 public 历史不会因为以后改可见性而自动消失。
