# CochraneK/ming audit — 2026-09-17

Audited commit: `d696a7e785879876a67cbc70cada5fe5182e2eb1`

## Decision

**STOP active development · maintenance only.** The project is complete enough to freeze. Resume for real user-facing bugs, historical/data corrections, or compatibility/dependency breakage; do not redesign the UI without concrete usability evidence.

## Product review

The repository now forms a coherent digital-humanities product rather than a visualization demo: people, places, events, relationships, maps, timelines, emperors, chronology, insights, search, details, deep links and offline delivery are integrated. The paper/ink/cinnabar/gold visual system is distinctive and internally consistent. Continued speculative polish has low expected user value.

The highest-value future work is content quality: correcting dates, places, relationships and extraction errors when evidence appears. Historical uncertainty should continue to be represented explicitly rather than filled with false precision.

## Evidence boundary

The CI workflow definition is mature, but an exact green combined status for the audited HEAD was not independently observed during this review. This audit is not copyright/legal clearance, and historical facts were not exhaustively verified entity by entity.

## Recheck triggers

Re-audit when a real bug is reported, a historical/data error is demonstrated, browser/Pages/service-worker/dependency changes break behavior, concrete usability evidence shows navigation failure, or the project starts accepting external contributions/formal releases.

## Publication gate

Current visibility is public. No new publication blocker was identified. Continue avoiding committed source-book full text and preserve the structured/derived-data boundary. This is not legal clearance.
